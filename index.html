About Mentor Campus

Connecting campus classrooms with real-world industry leaders.

MentorCampus (mentorcampus.in) solves the isolation between college academic curricula and real industry expectations. Instead of rare, formal guest lectures, we institutionalize ongoing access to the people driving modern technology, startups, and institutions.

Through student-led chapters, faculties and student core teams host recurring monthly themes, giving students a head start on real workplace readiness.
